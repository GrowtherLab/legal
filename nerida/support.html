Nerida Support

Nerida — Last updated: 5 November 2026

Nerida is a symptom diary for perimenopause and menopause. If something is not working, or you want a symptom or a feature that is missing, write to us — the address is at the bottom of this page and a person reads it.

Common questions

Why does Nerida say nothing for the first two weeks?

Patterns drawn from fewer than about fourteen days of entries are mostly noise. Nerida waits rather than showing you a finding it cannot stand behind. Keep logging and the insights appear on their own.

Where is my data stored? Can I get it back if I change phone?

Your entries are stored on your device only; Nerida has no account and no server. That means nothing of yours is uploaded, and it also means your history moves with your device backup. If you restore a new phone from an iCloud or computer backup that includes Nerida, your log comes with it. If you delete the app, the entries go with it.

I logged the wrong day or the wrong severity.

Open the day from the timeline and change any entry. Nothing is locked after the fact, and recalculating the patterns happens automatically.

What is "load"?

Load is simply the sum of the severities you recorded that day. It is Nerida's own number for spotting change over time. It is not a medical score and it does not mean anything outside your own log.

How do I share the report with my doctor?

The report builds once you have about two weeks of entries. It is a single page: your average daily load, your most present symptoms, the intervals between bleeds, and treatment before-and-after. Export it as a PDF and send or print it. It is generated on your device.

What does the subscription include, and what stays free?

Logging is free forever, including everything you have already written down. A subscription adds your full history beyond the last two weeks, every pattern Nerida finds instead of only the first, the one-page PDF for your doctor, and treatment before-and-after.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Cancel at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone. Cancelling stops the renewal; your logged entries stay on your device either way.

I paid but premium is not unlocked.

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. This asks Apple to re-check your account. If it still does not unlock, write to us with the date of purchase and we will look into it.

Is Nerida medical advice?

No. Nerida is a diary, not a diagnosis. It describes what you logged. It does not interpret your symptoms, does not recommend treatment, and a pattern it shows is not evidence of a cause. Talk to a clinician about anything that worries you.

Reporting a problem

It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the app version from the bottom of the settings screen, and what you were doing when it happened. Please do not send screenshots of your symptom entries unless they are necessary — we do not need your health data to fix a bug.
