Privacy Policy

Nerida — Last updated: 5 November 2026

Nerida is a symptom diary for perimenopause and menopause. This policy explains what the app handles and what leaves your device. Nerida has no user accounts, operates no servers of its own, and makes no network requests other than Apple's own purchase checks.

Your health entries stay on your phone

Everything you log is written to a database on your device and read back from there. Nerida contains no networking code for your entries: there is no server to send them to, no account to attach them to, and no export that happens without you asking for it. The one-page report is generated on your device, and it leaves only if you share it yourself.

Because the entries live on your device, they are covered by your device passcode and by your own iCloud or local backups, whichever you have enabled. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Nerida never sees or receives your payment details. The app asks Apple's StoreKit whether a subscription is currently active and uses nothing else to do so — there is no third-party subscription or analytics service in Nerida. See the Apple Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

  • Apple — payment processing and subscription billing

There are no others. Nerida sends no requests to any service operated by GrowtherLab or by anyone else.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-up or login
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone, location or Apple Health
  • No upload of your symptom history, in any form, to anywhere

Data retention

Your entries and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Because Nerida holds no accounts and runs no servers, there is no stored profile to delete or export.

Children

Nerida is intended for adults tracking perimenopause and menopause symptoms and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Medical disclaimer

Nerida is a diary, not a diagnosis. It describes what you logged. It does not interpret your symptoms, does not recommend treatment, and a pattern it shows is not evidence of a cause. Talk to a clinician about anything that worries you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As described above, Nerida does not hold personal data on any server; your entries are on your device and under your control. For questions about data handled by Apple, please refer to their policy linked above, or contact us.
