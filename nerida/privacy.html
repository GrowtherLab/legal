Privacy Policy

Nerida — Last updated: 24 November 2026

Nerida is a symptom diary for perimenopause and menopause. This policy explains what the app handles and what leaves your device. Nerida has no user accounts and operates no servers of its own. What you log, and anything the app reads from Apple Health, stays on your device. The only information that leaves it concerns the subscription: purchases are handled by Apple, and the subscription offer and status by Adapty.

Your health entries stay on your phone

Everything you log is written to a database on your device and read back from there. Nerida contains no networking code for your entries: there is no server to send them to and no account to attach them to. Neither Apple nor Adapty receives any symptom, bleeding, treatment or note you record. The one-page report and the spreadsheet export are generated on your device, and they leave it only if you share them yourself.

Because the entries live on your device, they are covered by your device passcode and by your own iCloud or local backups, whichever you have enabled. You can erase everything at any time with Settings › Delete all my data in the app, or by deleting the app.

Apple Health

Connecting Apple Health is optional and off until you turn it on. If you do, Nerida asks for permission to read sleep, sleeping wrist temperature, resting heart rate, heart rate variability and menstrual flow. You choose which of these to allow, and you can change that at any time in the Health app.

  • Nerida only reads from Apple Health. It never writes to it.
  • The readings are used on your device to show the measurements beside your entries and to tell you when a symptom lines up with one of them, in the app and in your report.
  • They are held in memory while the app is open. They are not copied into Nerida's own storage, not included in backups made by Nerida, and not sent to any server.
  • Apple Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with Adapty or any other third party.

If you share the report or the spreadsheet export yourself, it contains what you chose to include and goes only where you send it.

Reminders

If you switch on the daily reminder, it is scheduled by your phone for itself. No notification is sent from a server, and the reminder's wording does not mention any symptom or condition.

Purchases and subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Nerida never sees or receives your payment details. The app uses Adapty to show the subscription offer and to check whether a subscription is active. Adapty assigns an anonymous identifier to the installation, receives purchase events from Apple, and records when the subscription screen is shown together with basic device information (device model, iOS version and language). This identifier is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and it is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps. Collection of the advertising identifier and of your IP address by the Adapty SDK is switched off in Nerida. See the Adapty Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

  • Adapty — subscription offer and subscription status
  • Apple — payment processing and subscription billing

These services receive only the subscription data described in the table above, never your entries or your Apple Health data. Requests to them travel over the internet like any other connection.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-up or login
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No access to contacts, photos, microphone or location
  • No upload of your symptom history or Apple Health data, in any form, to anywhere

Data retention

Your entries and settings are stored only on your device and are removed when you use Delete all my data or delete the app. Nerida holds no accounts and runs no servers, so there is no profile of ours to delete or export. Data held by Adapty and Apple about your subscription is kept under their policies linked above.

Children

Nerida is intended for adults tracking perimenopause and menopause symptoms and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Medical disclaimer

Nerida is a diary, not a diagnosis. It describes what you logged. It does not interpret your symptoms, does not recommend treatment, and a pattern it shows is not evidence of a cause. Talk to a clinician about anything that worries you.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As described above, Nerida does not hold your entries on any server; they are on your device and under your control. For subscription data handled by Apple or Adapty, please refer to their policies linked above, or contact us and we will help you make the request.
