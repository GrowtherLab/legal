Privacy Policy

Iman Pro — Last updated: 2 August 2026

Iman Pro provides prayer times, athan notifications, a qibla compass and a monthly timetable. This policy explains what the app handles and what leaves your device. Iman Pro has no user accounts and operates no servers of its own.

Information the app handles

DataPurposeWhere it goes
Device location Determining your city so prayer times are accurate Used on your device only. Precise coordinates are never transmitted.
City and country name Requesting prayer times and the monthly timetable Sent to the Aladhan prayer times API
City name you type when searching Finding a location manually Sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding API
Calendar date Converting between Gregorian and Hijri dates Sent to the Aladhan API
Selected location and app settings Remembering your preferences Stored locally on your device
Subscription status Unlocking premium features Handled by Apple and by Adapty using an anonymous identifier

Location

If you grant location permission, Iman Pro uses your device location while the app is in use to work out which city you are in. Only the resulting city and country names are sent to the prayer times service. Your latitude and longitude are not transmitted and are not stored on any server. You can also skip location permission entirely and choose your city manually.

Notifications

Athan and prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent through external servers.

Purchases and subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Iman Pro never sees or receives your payment details. The app uses Adapty to check whether a subscription is active; Adapty assigns an anonymous identifier to the installation and receives purchase events from Apple. See the Adapty Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

  • Aladhan — prayer time and Hijri date calculations
  • Open-Meteo — city search (geocoding)
  • Adapty — subscription status management
  • Apple — payment processing and subscription billing

These services receive only the data described in the table above. Requests may include your device's IP address, as with any internet connection.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-up or login
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or behavioural tracking SDKs
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or health data access

Data retention

Your settings and selected location are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Because Iman Pro holds no accounts and runs no servers, there is no stored profile to delete or export.

Children

Iman Pro is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As described above, Iman Pro does not hold personal data on any server. For questions about data handled by Apple or Adapty, please refer to their policies linked above, or contact us.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
