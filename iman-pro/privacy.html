Privacy Policy

Salah Times, Athan & Qibla — Last updated: 23 November 2026

Salah Times provides prayer times, athan notifications, a qibla compass and a monthly timetable. On your phone it may be named Namaz Vakti, Waktu Solat, Waktu Sholat or مواقيت الصلاة, depending on your language; it was previously published under the name Iman Pro. This policy explains what the app handles and what leaves your device. Salah Times has no user accounts and operates no servers of its own.

Information the app handles

DataPurposeWhere it goes
Device location Working out your city, your official prayer-time zone and the qibla direction Used on your device only. Precise coordinates are never transmitted.
City and country name, and the date Requesting prayer times, the monthly timetable and Hijri dates Sent to the Aladhan prayer times API
Official zone or city code, and the date (Malaysia, Indonesia and Oman only) Requesting the timetable published by the national authority Sent to JAKIM (e-solat.gov.my) in Malaysia, to the myQuran API that serves the Kemenag timetable in Indonesia, or to the Ministry of Awqaf and Religious Affairs (mara.gov.om) in Oman. The code is chosen on your device from your location.
City name you type when searching Finding a location manually Sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding API
Selected location and app settings Remembering your preferences Stored locally on your device
Subscription status and purchase history Unlocking premium features Handled by Apple and by Adapty, linked to an anonymous identifier
Subscription screen views, device model, iOS version and language Showing and measuring the subscription offer Sent to Adapty, linked to the same anonymous identifier

Location

If you grant location permission, Salah Times uses your device location while the app is in use to work out which city you are in and the direction of the qibla. The qibla direction is calculated on your device. Only the resulting city and country names, or the official zone or city code where a national timetable is used, are sent to the prayer times services. Your latitude and longitude are not transmitted and are not stored on any server. You can also skip location permission entirely and choose your city manually.

Notifications

Athan and prayer reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification content is sent through external servers.

Purchases and subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple. Salah Times never sees or receives your payment details. The app uses Adapty to show the subscription offer and to check whether a subscription is active. Adapty assigns an anonymous identifier to the installation, receives purchase events from Apple, and records when the subscription screen is shown together with basic device information (device model, iOS version and language). This identifier is not linked to your name, email or Apple Account, and it is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps. See the Adapty Privacy Policy and the Apple Privacy Policy.

Third-party services

  • Aladhan — prayer time and Hijri date calculations
  • JAKIM e-Solat — official prayer times in Malaysia
  • myQuran — the Kemenag prayer timetable in Indonesia
  • Ministry of Awqaf and Religious Affairs, Oman — official prayer times in Oman
  • Open-Meteo — city search (geocoding)
  • Adapty — subscription offer and subscription status
  • Apple — payment processing and subscription billing

These services receive only the data described in the table above. Requests may include your device's IP address, as with any internet connection.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-up or login
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No analytics beyond the subscription screen views described above
  • No selling or sharing of personal data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or health data access

Data retention

Your settings and selected location are stored only on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Salah Times holds no accounts and runs no servers, so there is no profile of ours to delete or export. Data held by Adapty and Apple is kept under their policies linked above.

Children

Salah Times is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data held about you. As described above, Salah Times does not hold personal data on any server of its own. For questions about data handled by Apple or Adapty, please refer to their policies linked above, or contact us.
