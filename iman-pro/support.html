Salah Times Support

Salah Times, Athan & Qibla — Last updated: 23 November 2026

Salah Times shows the daily prayer times for where you are, plays the athan at each prayer, points you to the qibla, and keeps a monthly timetable. On your phone it may be named Namaz Vakti, Waktu Solat, Waktu Sholat or مواقيت الصلاة, depending on your language. If something is not working, write to us — the address is at the bottom of this page.

Common questions

My prayer times differ from my local mosque.

Prayer times are calculated from your location with a calculation method. By default the app picks the method used in your country; you can choose another under Calculation Method on the main screen. In Malaysia, Indonesia and Oman the app uses the timetable published by the national authority (JAKIM, Kemenag and the Ministry of Awqaf and Religious Affairs) instead of a calculation. Some mosques add their own margin of a few minutes; if yours does, follow your mosque.

The location shown is wrong.

Tap the place name on the main screen to search for your city, or switch back to automatic location. Automatic location needs location access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services. The app checks your position again each time you open it, so the times follow you when you travel.

I don't hear the athan.

Make sure notifications are on in the app's settings and in Settings › Notifications on your iPhone, and that the phone is not in Silent mode or a Focus that blocks notifications. The athan is scheduled several days ahead; opening the app now and then keeps the schedule up to date.

The qibla compass points the wrong way.

Hold the phone flat, away from magnets, metal and other electronics, and move it in a figure eight to calibrate the compass. Location access must be on for the qibla direction to be calculated for where you are.

How do I change the language?

Open settings from the main screen and choose Language. The app is available in English, Türkçe, Bahasa Melayu, Bahasa Indonesia and العربية. Arabic is laid out right to left; close and reopen the app after switching to or from it.

What does the subscription include, and what stays free?

Today's prayer times, the athan, the qibla compass and the Ramadan countdown are free. A subscription unlocks the full monthly timetable for every day and month of the year.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us. Cancel at any time in Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions on your iPhone. If you cancel during a free trial, you are not charged.

I paid but premium is not unlocked.

Open settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. This asks Apple to re-check your account. If it still does not unlock, write to us with the date of purchase and we will look into it.

Reporting a problem

It helps if you include your iPhone model, your iOS version, the city the app shows, and what you were doing when it happened.
